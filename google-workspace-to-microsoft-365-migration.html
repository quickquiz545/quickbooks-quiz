Why Move from Google Workspace to Microsoft 365?

  • Enhanced governance, DLP, and eDiscovery aligned with Microsoft Purview.
  • Deep integration with Windows, Azure AD, and desktop productivity apps.
  • Unified collaboration with Teams, SharePoint, and OneDrive for Business.
  • Consistent licensing, identity, and endpoint management across the estate.

We plan migrations that respect both IT guardrails and business expectations. From domain cutovers to permission mapping, our team ensures your users gain the full productivity value of Microsoft 365 without losing critical history.

Scope of Migration – What We Move

Gmail → Exchange Online

Mailbox data, labels to folders, and retention considerations.

Google Drive / Shared Drives → OneDrive & SharePoint

Shared ownership, permissions, and sharing links handled carefully.

Google Calendar → Outlook Calendar

Recurring meetings, resources, and delegated access kept intact.

Google Contacts → Outlook Contacts

Personal contacts, labels, and photo metadata aligned.

Google Chat, Spaces → Teams

Channel conversations and files mapped to Teams channels.

Google Groups → M365 Groups / Distribution Lists

Memberships synchronized with identity sources and rules.
